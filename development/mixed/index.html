For a five-item check, key MC1 = A, MC7 = C, NR1 = 4, NR2 = 7, and NR3 = 0. Scan the completed sheet and expect 1/5 with one response pending. Correct MC1 to A and resolve NR1 to reach 3/5. The local exports preserve original reads and corrections.

Known limits

Faint print can require manual authoring. Dense printed digits and independently photographed pen or pencil marks sometimes trigger unnecessary review. In a difficult independent reserve, exact NR observations including the expected review state were 8/16 using automatic corners and 14/16 using accurate manual corners. All valid captures registered and the shifted-row control was refused in both modes.

Independent browser testing covered mixed keys, proficiency and outcome subtotals, recognition, corrections, local persistence and backup restore. Physical iPhone Safari acceptance remains pending.
