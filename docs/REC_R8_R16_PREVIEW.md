## What changed

- **Local NR geometry.** The REC-R9 bounded slot translation uses only printed ring and exterior evidence. Its two alternating subsets must corroborate a correction. A REC-R10 audit then checks every mapped bubble around the selected position, so a single displaced row can withhold the slot. The preview distinguishes supported original and corrected positions, unreliable geometry, and unobservable geometry. The existing pose and handwriting regions remain intact for Established and baseline comparison.
- **Independent reference check.** Blank NR patches have ring and exterior evidence audited at their enrolled positions. The scan fit cannot correct the reference. MC retains its conservative ink check. A missing, displaced or otherwise unsupported reference patch withholds reference-conditioned decisions for that group. Teacher confirmation is still needed for blankness. Similar printed structures can pass a ring check, so layout compatibility continues to rely on independent existing registration safeguards.
- **Compact references.** A revision-bound v2 record adds explicit sampler and pose provenance to the existing integrity check. New NR patches are audited once at enrollment. Legacy compact and JPEG references are still supported and undergo the applicable checks during comparison. An accidental-corruption checksum does not authenticate a reference.
- **Guarded decisions.** Experimental agreement and stacking verify expected group and symbol identities and complete optical evidence. Conflicting and incomplete observations are withheld. NR slot decisions require supported geometry and an independently supported reference. Any confident handwriting must agree with an existing optical reading. Ordered unique slot identities and number format are checked before an NR candidate is shown.
