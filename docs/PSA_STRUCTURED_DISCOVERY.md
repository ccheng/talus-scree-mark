# PSA Structured discovery

The blank-template editor runs Established when a blank image is opened. Once the paper is aligned, **Find all blocks with PSA Structured** runs the Established pass plus local PSA MC and Alberta NR workers. Use **Find blocks outside exclusions** to rerun Established. Release kept blocks before comparing engines, or open the same blank in a fresh draft for each run. Use the same corners and exclusions and export diagnostics after each run.

Enter an expected MC count when known, including for mixed sheets. Check printed numbers and symbols before saving. The row controls within each MC block remove or insert one row without renumbering or moving its neighbours. Use **Renumber block** or **Edit numbering and printed symbols** where the printed IDs differ. For an insertion, box only the new row, enter its printed number, and find or place the bubbles before confirming. Use **Show region** for a competing interpretation, then remove an incorrect proposal or use the bounded controls to recover the correct region. The bounded Alberta selector still offers both engines.

The diagnostic JSON is downloaded locally. It includes draft geometry in Letter points, item IDs, printed symbols, placement provenance, and raw proposal and arbitration evidence. PSA’s OCR numbers are suggestions. Structural confidence and placement provenance are distinct. Legacy profiles without explicit placement provenance keep their geometry and continue to load.

## Scope and evidence

- Source is the [MC-R25 handoff](https://github.com/ccheng/talus-scree-mark/blob/4d7c41d794a9236e59c67250d073e8d73a189550/research/psa-mc/MC-R25-HANDOFF.md). The bundled MC glyph bank is byte-identical to that handoff. The two workers share the same rectified raster coordinates for arbitration. Proposal centers and radii are converted to page points once. Where the engines agree on MC geometry, the editor retains the Established centers. Added MC proposals require review.
- Independent Chromium testing used frozen, privately held synthetic photographs and independent bubble centers. Both engines mapped 168 of 168 true MC centers and 210 of 210 true NR centers within 2 points across three blank images. PSA withheld one false Established NR grid in the checklist control and signalled a conflict. PSA NR center error at the 95th percentile was 0.26, 0.32 and 0.41 points across the three images, versus 0.45, 0.41 and 0.55 points with Established.
- On a separate synthetic 18-question sheet with broken outlines, 4- and 5-choice rows and uneven lighting, PSA matched 13 true rows and 59 of 84 symbol-aligned centers within 2 points. Established matched 5 true rows and 20 centers. PSA still missed five true rows and proposed six unmatched ones. The expected count and printed ID review matter on this layout.
- Both engines proposed a 15th MC row on a 14-question sheet. The expected-count check blocked saving. The tester deleted and reinserted a correct row, confirmed its four printed centers, and verified exact preservation of every other bubble. Removing the false row and reviewing numbering produced all 14 MC and two NR IDs. The corrected template saved and reopened offline.
- The held-out completed-sheet check scored a perfect matching sheet 16/16, routed three ambiguous entries to review on a second sheet, and persisted a corrected score of 14/16. A shifted wrong layout required retake with zero observations. CSV and photo-inclusive backup restore passed in a fresh browser profile. After caching, offline checks blocked origin asset responses and saw only Chromium’s service-worker script update probes reach the test origin. Cancel and forced MC bank unavailability preserved Established geometry. A kept MC block and an NR exclusion were respected.

This is a synthetic development release. Physical iPhone Safari and installed PWA tests, memory and latency on device, and comparison on the original Physics photograph remain open. The benchmark is too small for a general engine ranking. Native detection can still miss a faint row or suggest an extra one. Confirm geometry, printed IDs and symbols on the sheet before using a template for grading.
