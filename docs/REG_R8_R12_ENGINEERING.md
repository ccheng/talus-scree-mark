## Selected engineering changes

- Version 3 shadow enrollment records tentative two-ring footprints and ambiguous measured footprints separately from other row evidence. Version 1 and 2 remain readable. Bounded geometric arbitration selects disjoint multi-ring hypotheses where evidence supports a clear solution, and retains near-ties and search-budget exhaustion as ambiguity. Tentative pairs never become required rows, scope exemptions or registration vetoes. Structural coverage stays bounded and whole-sheet compatibility remains unknown.
- Teacher diagnostics preserve the Established retake or wrong-batch action. Successful reads can carry at most three localized inspection suggestions at a time. Unclassified structure, competing associations and print differences are distinct observations. A novel tentative pair can still surface when unrelated ambiguities make the inventory incomplete. Re-observed ambiguous footprints remain in diagnostic evidence and are suppressed from prompts only where matched to the enrolled reference.
- The first candidate could hide a real pair location behind unrelated ambiguous print and prompt on recurring footer ambiguity. The bounded correction addresses the location and recurrence independently. It leaves recognition and compatibility decisions unchanged.
- The glyph-bank compatibility experiment, directional damage rules and automatic pose override were declined. Local recovery and component-first enrollment were replayed comparatively. Neither was added to the default candidate.
