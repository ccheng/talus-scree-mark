# PSA Structured template authoring corrections

[Open the development preview](https://ccheng.github.io/talus-scree-mark/cpas-preview/?cpas=preview). Import the blank and choose **PSA Structured** in the template editor. Compare the overlay against the physical sheet before saving.

- **Provisional numerical grids.** Strong, incomplete header-based Alberta NR grids remain visible with all 42 estimated positions. Inspect each grid against the print, then choose **Confirm NR grid**, edit the mapping, or remove the grid. The saved geometry retains inferred provenance. Weaker or invalid proposals are listed for inspection. Bounded fits and existing registration checks retain their prior admission rules.
- **Printed question numbers.** Confirmed, unique printed MC numbers follow their rows, including nonconsecutive numbers. Display order remains independent. An unverified number requires explicit confirmation. For conflicting claims, choose **Edit numbering and symbols** and resolve the affected rows. Draft fallback IDs are never accepted by a generic discovery acknowledgment.
- **Unresolved regions.** Inspect the region and confirm the intended overlapping mapping, or remove the proposal and explicitly omit the region. Enter **Expected MC questions** when known to check coverage. A discovery pass cannot certify that it found every printed question.
- **Persistence.** Review the saved template and associate its key by the confirmed question IDs. The app uses the same grading and scan registration rules.
