# Milestone 3 specification — Reusable profiles for existing answer sheets

**Status:** Approved for implementation after SWE Director and Product Owner review. Acceptance and merge require separate Product Owner approval.

## Outcome and scope

A teacher imports a blank copy of an existing answer sheet, sets up its MC and NR blocks, confirms a reusable profile and scans marked copies against that profile. Existing sheets need no special printed markers or redesign. Support multiple MC and NR blocks, different placements and order, per-item MC choice maps and four-position NR grids with per-slot symbols. Show unsupported structures and invalid maps before confirmation. Keep M2 recognized exactly as before, through its coded reference strategy. Automatic discovery of unknown layouts across an entire page, grading, answer keys, correction histories, OCR and persistence of student sheets or recognition results remain outside M3.

## Teacher workflow and profile contract

1. Import a blank JPEG/PNG through the existing local image path. Investigate local single-page PDF rasterization. PDF support is nonblocking if it substantially increases implementation complexity. Propose page corners where confident and let the teacher adjust them in zoomable preview. Preserve the page’s upright orientation and canonical frame.
2. Select an MC or NR block and draw a region. Propose a bubble lattice inside it. Show every proposed bubble and its logical item, choice or slot. Let the teacher confirm item order, numbering/prefix, choices, symbol labels and NR slot direction. Support three calibration levels: automatic proposal in the selected region, parametric grid calibration with endpoints, row/column counts, spacing and orientation, and individual bubble correction for exceptions. A full grid is not built one bubble at a time in the ordinary workflow. Support stacked and side-by-side blocks without cross-block ordering assumptions.
3. Validate unique IDs, non-overlapping valid regions, centers and radii, item order and unambiguous symbol-to-cell mappings. Reject malformed, incompatible or incomplete layouts before saving or scoring. Require explicit confirmation of the preview and review schema changes before activating a new revision.
4. Save the confirmed profile locally for offline reuse. Use immutable profile ID and revision, canonical dimensions and orientation, ordered component and geometry maps, registration strategy, checks and provenance. Allow selection, rename, deliberate revision and deletion. Persist only profile data and minimized printed-feature descriptors. Keep blank and marked images, names, answers and results ephemeral. Exclude student/name fields and answer pixels from registration descriptors. Invalidate in-flight and previous results on revision change, profile switch, retake or clear.
5. Select an exact profile revision once, then capture or import successive marked sheets. Keep the selection throughout that session and rerun registration, identity and geometry checks for every capture. A geometric layout match verifies the reusable layout only. The assessment version and answer-key identity are separate; shared geometry never selects or proves either. An indistinguishable form may require explicit per-scan identity confirmation as described below. Show the verified registration strategy and location evidence, literal observations, review reasons, and retake guidance. Reuse M2 item statuses, exact four-slot NR values, clear-blank handling and conservative review behavior.

## Semi-automatic authoring investigation

Compare two practical pipelines on the same independently labeled blank forms. Start with local grayscale normalization and connected components or ring candidates filtered for plausible size, aspect and outline. Compare multi-threshold candidate extraction or a circle/edge alternative when faint outlines, text or grid lines degrade the baseline. Group candidates by robust spacing, row/column alignment and radius within each teacher-selected block. Check uniqueness, missing/extra cells and residuals. Show incomplete or competing patterns for correction. Avoid inventing bubble locations or semantics. A teacher can always complete the profile using manual grid placement and per-cell edits.

Keep candidate detection, regular-grid fitting and semantic assignment separate. The detector proposes geometry, the teacher supplies item mapping, and the reading worker consumes only a validated profile. This interface leaves future automatic layout discovery able to supply candidate regions without changing the reader. Favor a local JavaScript approach first and justify any added dependency using measured improvement, offline size and testability.

Measure proposals before edits against labeled centers and groups. Report precision, recall, duplicates, missing bubbles, center displacement in local radii and exact semantic mappings after confirmation. Compare assisted and manual workflows on equivalent blank forms with counterbalanced order where feasible. Report attempts, retries, corrections and time to confirmed profile. Use approximately two minutes for straightforward MC setup and five minutes for typical mixed MC/NR setup as provisional usability objectives. Measure from opening a prepared blank image to saving a confirmed profile, report manual corrections and retries, and distinguish scripted time from actual teacher setup time. A usability target miss is nonblocking. A wrong saved mapping is a correctness failure even when proposals are fast.

## Markerless registration and safety

Use the selected profile, page boundaries and independent fixed printed features. Propose an orientation-aware quadrilateral from visible page edges, with manual corner placement as fallback. Reuse the projective transform and verify or refine it using distributed, distinctive printed landmarks or constrained feature matches outside response/name areas. Check match uniqueness, geometry residuals and coverage across blocks. Use boundaries for fit and separate interior evidence for verification. Corner fits and repeated bubble patterns alone are insufficient to establish layout identity.

Fail closed on unreliable geometry, clipping, unknown or wrong profile, mirrored orientation and ambiguous mappings. Where visually indistinguishable variants cannot be distinguished by image evidence, block automatic acceptance or require an explicit per-scan teacher identity decision. Display and report that pathway separately from automatic verification. A saved profile, manual corners or a selected revision do not by themselves verify a capture. If local evidence is unreadable after valid registration, withhold that item. Clear evidence of double marks remains a mandatory review. Keep M2 coded registration as a distinct strategy using the common result and reader interfaces.

## Acceptance and evaluation

| Gate | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Permanent M1/M2 regression | Run both existing Chromium workflows and Node suites. Keep the published M2 profile digest and a frozen known-answer fixture/key as compatibility oracles. Retain 20 standard cases and existing hard negatives, literal outputs, geometry, retake, offline and privacy checks. A new or regenerated profile cannot replace the frozen oracle. |
| Profile reuse and variable layout | At least three structurally distinct layout families, including the M2 anchored control and realistic markerless MC-only, mixed MC/NR, and interleaved-block layouts. Include an existing-style blank import, manual and assisted authoring, and a genuinely new family enrolled after tuning freezes using only its blank. Verify exact mapping, offline reload, chosen revision and failure on invalid/stale profiles. Score fresh marked captures the author has not seen. |
| Registration and rejection | Test wrong same-size or lookalike forms, rotation, mirror, tilt, crop, missing blocks, low-contrast edges and manual-corner fallback. Independently label check points and bubble centers distributed across all blocks. Report normalized displacement and rejection by strategy/family. Seeded wrong or unconfirmed profiles, unresolved identity and unusable registration must yield no automatically accepted results. |
| Synthetic benchmark | At least eight standard captures and four stress/negative captures per family. Freeze common settings, test oracles and thresholds before holdout scoring. Report exact responses, coverage, silent errors and retakes separately by family, MC/NR and path. Report automatic, human-confirmed, reviewed and retake paths separately. Include frozen M2 geometry gates. |
| Physical workflow | Test the published exact build on named iPhone hardware in Safari and Home Screen, including blank import, confirmation, offline profile reload, camera, adjudication and retake. Record installed/offline mode and checks run. |
| Representative physical benchmark | Schedule after representative M3 layouts are available. Plan approximately 30–40 distinct adult-marked mock sheets across the three families, covering print, lighting and capture conditions. Prefer two named iPhones if available. Use one standard attempt per sheet per preregistered mode, keep repeated attempts with their sheet, writer and session, and score stress separately. Report exact matches, response and cluster counts, automatic coverage and silent error among both accepted and planned items, uncertainty by independent sampling unit, and cold/warm scan-to-painted-result latency. |

Retain the provisional engineering targets of at least 95% automatic coverage, at most 0.5% silent error among accepted responses, and physical iPhone p95 at most 3 seconds. Freeze their interpretation before scoring and report each relevant stratum with counts and uncertainty. Include planned items on retaken sheets in coverage. Keep a missing performance target distinct from a blocking correctness failure. Report sparse or all-zero physical error samples at the supported unit and avoid asserting population reliability from small or dependent samples.

Use synthetic mock sheets only on the public GitHub Pages preview, with offline local processing and verified candidate asset delivery. Developer implements after approval. Independent Tester owns acceptance oracles and regression checks. Research Evaluator controls sealed scoring. Allow one bounded correction with focused retest and reassessment as needed. Product Owner decides acceptance and merge separately. Report the exact deployed preview URL and build, evidence, limitations and outstanding gates. Provide accessible printable materials and a practical iPhone walkthrough for physical benchmarking.
