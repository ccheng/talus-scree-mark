## Experiment
18 authored synthetic document families, 3 captures of 16 MC items per family, 864 item observations. Separate by family: train 0–8 (432), calibration 9–12 (192), evaluation 13–17 (240). Variations in radius, spacing, choice count, illumination, stroke pattern and imposed sampling displacement (not actual registration accuracy). All from same generator and balanced strict-policy classes; not representative prevalence. One candidate at most: existing definitive Established or CPAS optical literal, with conflict, competing, unobservable and missing reference safeguards. Training 317 candidate rows (194 eligible correct, 123 invalid). 20 feature inputs (some duplicate/constant in this pilot), standardized regularized binary logistic regression lambda=.05, 1800 gradient steps. No answer key/ground truth feature. Score estimates acceptability under experimental labels; not calibrated real-world probability. Threshold .7862137339932567 selected solely on calibration set by max negative score plus 1e-8, floor .5; calibration 65/96 correct, 0/96 policy violations. Evaluation held out from fit/threshold; inspect only after freezing.

- Strict agreement 74 / 0 / 12 / 154.
- Same optical without CPAS confidence gate 95 / 0 / 14 / 131.

The one stack violation was ghost marked B (family14 capture0 MC15), score .8037004021381037. Light eligible 4/30 stack vs 22/30 CPAS and 14/30 Established; blank 28/30 stack vs 22/30 CPAS. Thus aggregate gain conceals light-mark loss. At threshold .5 held-out 109/120 correct and 19/120 violations; .9 yields 27/120 and 0/120; these are descriptive held-out curves, not grounds for tuning. No evidence of reduced silent wrong-answer rate: all baselines also zero on eligible cases. Strict policy is experimental and may differ from PO policy. No comparison of true teacher workload, full-page failure, device time, or other NR/HWR evidence.
