## R8: controlled attribution
Native Established uses center/radius sampling and blankInk conditioning; CPAS samples 41x41 patches at the selected page/block transform and compares to separately registered blank. A geometry error can therefore mimic added ink or suppress valid evidence; correct geometry is not itself evidence of valid shading. Three authored raster families (1.5/2.4/3 px per point, radii 3.6/3.4/4.1 pt), nine scenarios, four digit slots each, 108 observations including 12 explicit registration rejection cases. Per scenario 9 eligible slots (dark 3, light 7, blank) and 3 half-shaded 8 controls requiring review. Same authored generator, fixed digit identities and mark shapes, seven-segment approximation, known true blank pose, no decimals or full-item format/admission; repeated observations not independent. Oracle uses known true translation only where all rings share it; unavailable for split-shift. Synthetic displacement is not measured field incidence.

On three ordinary shifted scenarios (small .6/-.36 pt, large 1.2/-.72 pt, differing translation by slot), 27 eligible and 9 review slots: baseline Established 13 correct, 0 wrong, 7 policy violations; oracle 20 correct, 0 wrong, 7 violations. CPAS literal baseline 4 correct, 0 wrong, 0 violations; oracle 27 correct, 0 wrong, 0 violations. Thus geometry alone explains substantial CPAS losses in this constructed experiment. Established continues accepting partial marks at true pose; no general conclusion about actual classroom accuracy. All CPAS NR measurements are literal advisory, not authoritative acceptance.

## R9: candidate
nr-local-pose.js estimates a common bounded page-coordinate translation per vertical NR slot, using median exterior-minus-ring contrast, no glyph core or mark prediction. Alternating ring subsets independently search a .08 radius grid within +/- .4 radius per axis; require subset agreement within .12 radius, no search boundary optimum, minimum exterior contrast .12, >=75% ring support and .025 gain over zero on both subsets. Abstain on rejected registration, malformed geometry, undersampling or insufficient consensus. These are exploratory design thresholds, not externally validated. Both subsets participate in estimating the proposal; they are not independent validation. Not a compatibility detector; no overrides or production policy changes.

Ordinary shifts: 36/36 proposals, mean ring-center error 1.097276 -> .099943 pt (max residual .154829 pt). Refined Established 19/27 correct, 0 wrong, 7/9 policy violations; refined CPAS 27/27 correct, 0 wrong, 0/9 violations. CPAS matches oracle count on ordinary cases. Faint outlines: 8/12 proposals, mean error 1 -> .404249 pt, CPAS correct 3/9 -> 6/9 (oracle 6/9); Established 0/9 -> 3/9, one review-policy violation newly accepted. Clean, contradictory alternating shifts, missing rings, beyond search bound and registration-rejected: 0/60 proposals; all unchanged baseline outputs identical and rejected cases had no recognition outputs. In split-shift baseline Established still produces one wrong eligible digit and three policy violations: abstaining from correction does NOT establish safe geometry. On 96 admitted synthetic slot observations (72 eligible, 24 review), Established correct 27->36, wrong 1->1, policy violations 13->14; CPAS literal correct 16->42, wrong 0->0, violations 0->0. These are exploratory slot-level measurements, not complete response or grading results.
