## Challenge
Four layouts, 13 scenarios each, 208 slot observations and 52 item/capture cases (48 admitted, 4 explicitly registration-rejected). Includes decimal choices, blank and nonblank full responses, one half-mark control per scenario, perspective/skew, illumination gradients, deterministic noise, blur, faint/broken outlines, low resolution, smooth warp, inconsistent row shifts, decoy rings, and separate reference drift. Seven-segment print approximation and repeated authored conditions, not independent samples. Decoy explicitly forced through registered status to probe a downstream safeguard; not a finding that actual registration admits this wrong layout. Full-item results aggregate native slot readings and a format rule; not authoritative app admission or grading. Geometry error threshold >0.5 page points is an exploratory diagnostic, not a validated safety limit.

## Results
Initial R10 candidate independently searches each mapped ring around R9 pose; marks site as unsupported if contrast insufficient or displaced optimum meaningfully outperforms origin. Initial >=90% site gate rejected smooth warp and two-row errors, but admitted all 16 single-row-shift cases. One bounded correction requires every mapped ring supported and independently checks the reference at its enrolled pose (no correction to the reference or stored blank calibration). The stricter variant was evaluated on the same open authored challenge, not held-out. All 16 reference-drift cases flagged.

Geometry: R9 proposed 9 smooth warp +16 one-row +16 two-row +16 decoy corrections with max residual >.5 pt (57 total). Strict R10 flagged all 41 warp/row cases, but still supported all 16 decoy-grid cases; cannot infer identity from ring evidence. Clean 16/16 supported, translation and slot drift each 16/16 supported, faint 12/16, broken 16/16, low-res 0/16, balanced split 0/16, rejected 0/16. No proposal on clean; no recognition on rejected. Mean translation error .949 -> .123 pt; slot drift 1.030 -> .133 pt. Reference audit detects imposed drift but does not correct it; no assertion that all bad references can be detected.

Full item literal results on 48 admitted cases (33 eligible,15 review controls including decoys): Established baseline 12 correct/0 wrong/7 review violations, R9 20/0/8, strict R10 10/0/4. CPAS baseline 8/0/0, R9 15/0/0, strict R10 14/0/0. In ordinary translation and slot drift alone CPAS 0/6 -> 6/6 under either refinement; no review violations. Geometry gate reduces Established acceptance but does not solve half-mark policy violations. No reduction in silent wrong-answer rate can be claimed (zero wrong eligible full items for all methods on this experiment). All decoy item paths withheld by both recognizers even though geometry audit failed to detect false target. No thresholds promoted.
