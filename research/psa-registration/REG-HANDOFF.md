## Latest development findings
18 targeted tests across three related layouts, all PSA-authored:
- Valid: candidate 7/9, baseline 7/9. Both 3/3 flat; cropped margins candidate 3/3 vs baseline 1/3; 90-degree candidate 1/3 vs baseline 3/3.
- Incompatible semantic controls: candidate withheld 9/9 (3 NR digit order, 3 NR decimal replacement, 3 MC choice order); baseline withheld 0/9. Changed number swap was separately reproduced on earlier consolidated revision, not included in the final 18.
- Enrollment: each layout 14/14 MC number sites, 9/9 header sites, 80/80 NR digit sites, 3/4 decimal sites. Earlier enrollment was 235/240 digit sites and 6/12 decimal sites; current digit and decimal checks actually consume enrolled sites. A missing site is unavailable evidence, not verified. A replacement of an unenrolled/masked decimal may escape detection; teacher-visible enrollment coverage is required before any claim of semantic verification.
- Seven accepted valid cases: 107/112 exact status/rawText matches; five additional NR review states; zero unexpected clear responses; all 7 grades 12/16 after simulated oracle adjudication. The intended double marks already require review; these five NR uncertainties are extra. This is actual grading contract execution in memory, not UI or persistence testing.
- Direct-source local affine mark sampling did not resolve low-resolution ambiguity and sometimes reduced exact observations on otherwise flat sheets. Do not substitute for rectified reading without evidence.
- 90-degree failures are false NR digit order conflicts even with good center geometry. Subpixel variants and independently corroborated baseline pose fallback did not solve them. Preserve withholding; do not tune thresholds simply to pass these fixtures. Investigate acquisition resolution, blank-ink calibration, sampling aliasing and coherent semantic evidence across slots using independent examples.
- Scope: consolidated revision tested added row without exclusion (withheld) and with explicit exclusion (accepted 16/16); separate MC-only fixture correctly distinguished all five scope dispositions, but existing baseline geometry gate rejected even enrolled out-of-scope row and fully excluded row. Requires coordinated scope-aware geometry verification; helper alone cannot override gate. Unknown additions on partial profiles are review, not auto-accepted. Complete profiles never automatically exempt an enrolled extra row.

## Recommended handoff boundary
1. ISTE should independently design heterogeneous synthetic/photographed fixtures before further tuning: MC-only, NR-only, mixed, variable rows/columns/typography, partial/excluded layouts, wrong semantics, filled/weak/multiple/erased marks, low-resolution rotated scans, crop content vs margins, paper deformation, mirrored and multiple sheets. Use known exact coordinates and semantic ground truth, compare actual baseline and frozen candidate, record localized errors, review burden, false clear/wrong layout, corrected grades and runtime. Clearly separate unsupported evidence from contradiction. Test single changed symbols as well as whole permutations.
2. LSDE may review and prepare a general-profile adapter on a development branch if PO separately authorizes integration. Preserve Established as initial baseline/fallback; never override verified multiple-sheet/mirror/batch/orientation safety veto or confirmed semantic conflict. Recovery only with independent geometric evidence and bounded transform; withhold ambiguous geometry. Do not silently adopt fixture-specific block counts/IDs/slots, coordinate locations, or magic thresholds. Enrollment must be versioned, validated, optional for legacy templates, locally stored and compatible with backup/restore. Unknown semantics, partial enrollment, exclusions and inferred/manual geometry require explicit policies. Coordinate the scope gate with existing response geometry and do not grant exemptions on the basis of a mere partial scope flag.
3. Candidate is NOT yet a deployable engine, and independent acceptance has not been performed. Need an actual general-profile runtime contract, broad regression and independent evaluation, browser/offline/worker limits, and device performance before deployment. Preserve item-level review semantics and no observations on incompatible sheets.

Recommendation: stop broad speculative registration R&D now. Freeze this evidence revision and ask ISTE to establish the next independent verification gate; use PSA for evidence-driven corrective work. LSDE can undertake bounded adapter work, not production replacement, after PO direction.
